# Three-zone Finnish–Swedish market corridor sensitivity — scientifically bounded

This model has THREE ABSTRACT MARKET BUSES: `SE1_supply`, `SE3_supply`, `FI_project`. Two independent, directed Swedish–Finnish links use **actual Fingrid historical 15-minute unused intraday commercial capacity after day-ahead trades** (dataset [38](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/38) SE1→FI and [39](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/39) SE3→FI), each as its own independent upper dispatch bound. Signed measured *commercial* history (datasets [31](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/31) and [32](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/32)) is historical context, not identical to physical AC/DC line flows.

Source has already been authenticated and SHA-256 verified in [Actions run 39420079414](https://github.com/abhijith-sivaprasadan/finnish-grid-connection-evidence/actions/runs/39420079414). The model must **reuse archived actual original 2025 CSVs**, never make new Fingrid API requests or infer missing values. Study window: September 15–October 15, 2025 UTC exclusive; 2,880 quarter-hours.

A three-bus PyPSA/HiGHS model with explicitly independent branch-constrained commercial links tests 24 cases: both available, SE1-only, or SE3-only; flat versus energy-neutral hindsight ±50 MW flexible 100 MW load; 0/2/4/8 h fictional 100 MW battery. Swedish supply behind each link, zero link transmission losses, and generator prices are all **assumptions**. Removing a corridor cannot improve the ideal minimum-penalty solution; this is tested.

**This is NOT a physical network-constrained AC/DC power-flow model**. Observed unused *intraday market* capacity is not total physical branch thermal rating, not a guaranteed project trade, and not operator-approved FI import headroom. PyPSA links are independent zonal trading abstractions with no internal Finnish substation topology, reactance, impedance, transient stability or N−1 security constraints.

Operator Fingrid documents the separate actual Finnish internal P1 north-south transmission cut at [dataset 30](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/30) and its planned weekly directional capacities in [28](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/28) and [29](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/datasets/29). But 30 has native 1-minute temporal resolution (MWh/h power equivalent), and 28/29 are **ended** datasets; their usable 2025 historical availability must be independently audited and **cannot be silently imported as 15-minute branch ratings**. Do not claim a physically network-constrained national model until current appropriate operator-sanctioned time series, geographic topology, component branch ratings, impedance/reactance and network validation exist. Alternative public approximate grid sources (PyPSA-Eur) are useful for non-authoritative exploratory studies only and require separate provenance and calibration review.

The solver's penalty relaxation is **never Finnish grid blackouts** and its SE1/SE3 trade dispatch split is model-dependent, not a historical observed allocation.
